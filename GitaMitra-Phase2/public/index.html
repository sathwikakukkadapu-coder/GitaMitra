TODAY'S VERSE · BHAGAVAD GITA 2.47
कर्मण्येवाधिकारस्ते मा फलेषु कदाचन।
मा कर्मफलहेतुर्भूर्मा ते सङ्गोऽस्त्वकर्मणि॥

karmaṇy-evādhikāras te mā phaleṣu kadācana

Meaning

You have a choice over your actions, but not ownership of their results. Do not let the outcome alone become your motive, and do not fall into inaction.

Take this into your day

Choose one meaningful task. Give it your full attention, do it sincerely, and let go of the urge to control every result.

Sanskrit verse · Bhagavad Gita 2.47. English rendering is an original plain-language translation.
